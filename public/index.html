Future Motion Makes Repairs Hard

A Onewheel that wouldn't turn on after sitting in a garage for months. The BMS was dead, an easy fix on paper. Future Motion's firmware locks replacement BMS units out with an 'incompatible hardware' error even from the same model board. The fix was an open-source OWIE chip that spoofs the RS485 handshake.
