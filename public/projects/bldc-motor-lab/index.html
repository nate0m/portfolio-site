Discovery

  • Gate voltage relationship to current through phase
  • Current relationship to angular velocity
  • Observation of BEMF
  • Analog vs digital signals at FET gates, for digital signals we think PWM would be necessary to modulate current / speed

MVP ‘minimum viable product’

Aka deliverables

  • Speed control via pwm
  • Ability to start from a stand still
  • Sensorless control (BEMF)
  • ESP32

How We gon’ get There?

  1. Look at the datasheet to determine the relationship between gate voltage and current through fet. Then verify this relationship in the lab by formulating an experiment and executing it.

  2. Rebuild crude BLDC controller with microcontroller programmed for constant angular velocity.

  3. Formulate and execute an experiment to measure the relationship between gate voltage and phase current, as well as angular velocity.

  4. Observe BEMF. Include relevant scope traces in your lab report. Yes there will be a lab report.

  5. Create a FSM based on your BEMF data to initiate motor rotation, accelerate, decelerate, stop, and rotate at a constant angular velocity.

  6. Design a BLDC controller circuit with the necessary components to observe BEMF (comparator) and the ability to accommodate either an analog or digital signal at the gate.

  7. Formulate and execute an experiment to test different ways of changing speeds via digital and analog signals. Document findings, what worked best and what you will implement for the final design.

  8. Redesign your BLDC controller and build it on a protoboard.
