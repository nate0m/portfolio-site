I think I figured out the light hiring for the harness

Pink - battery +

Brown - battery +

Orange - battery -

Wire with xt30 before anti spark to utilize battery fuse as safety mechanism

Harness to controller comm

Black - gnd

Red - 3.3v

Grey - 3.3v

Blue - adc1

Purple - adc2
